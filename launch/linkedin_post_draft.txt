I got tired of rewriting my resume for every JD.

So I built Resume Analyser.

Paste a Naukri or LinkedIn job screenshot. Upload your resume PDF. You'll get a full pack back:

1. ATS score and verdict
2. Missing keywords from the JD
3. Updated resume with those keywords woven in (your history stays. Nothing gets trimmed.)
4. Five professional PDF templates
5. A tailored cover letter
6. India Tier-1 / Tier-2 / Tier-3 salary bands for the role

I built this the same way I'm learning AI product work: ship something real, then improve it in public.

If you're applying this week, try it with one real JD and tell me where it still falls short.
